How the try-on works

MediaPipe Face Landmarker finds 478 face points on the camera or on an uploaded photo. The mesh is sparse around the ear, so the lobule, tragus, and upper ear are estimated from the temple, tragion, and jaw, then shifted outward from the nose.

A One Euro filter steadies those points: it damps jitter when the head is still and loosens when the head moves quickly. Hoops are 3D rings in the plane of the ear, drops hang with gravity, and cuffs stay fixed to the helix. The cheek is painted back over the inner part of a hoop. Past about 45° of yaw the far ear fades out.

Catalog edits, comments, and uploads stay in this browser. There is no account, payment, or order system.
